Write only the front matter (overview) for a business meeting document. Your source is the
numbered `### Section` blocks below: notes on consecutive segments of one meeting. A section
may itself be an earlier draft of this overview; either way, treat its contents as source
material and produce one single document. Never copy a `### Section N` heading into your
output.

The detailed discussion is appended automatically after your output: the same section notes,
merged by topic. Do not reproduce it here and do not write detailed topic sections.

Produce exactly this structure and nothing else:

1. `#` title — the meeting's actual subject, not "Business Meeting Notes". Never invent a date.
2. Italic one-line meeting summary
3. **Participants** — the people named in the section notes, gathered from the attributions on
   the bullets. Prefer display names when the notes have them.
4. **Primary topics** — comma-separated
5. `## Executive Summary` — a few paragraphs covering the arc of the whole discussion, leading
   with what was decided and what it costs or earns
6. `## Goals and KPIs` — bullets: each objective with the measure and target attached to it
7. `## Numbers and Budget` — bullets: every figure from the notes with its unit, period, and
   whether it is an actual, a forecast, or an estimate. Never round or invent a figure.
8. `## Stakeholders` — bullets: each person, team, partner, or customer named, and what they
   want or are owed
9. `## Timeline and Milestones` — bullets in date order, with what depends on each date
10. `## Key Decisions and Direction` — numbered list, every decision in the notes
11. `## Action Items` — Markdown pipe table with columns Owner | Action | Priority, every action
    and commitment in the notes
12. `## Open Questions` — bullet list
13. `## Risks, Dependencies, and Blockers` — include mitigation when it was discussed
14. `## Closing Assessment` — short

Consecutive sections overlap: when the same goal, figure, decision, action, question, or risk
appears in more than one section, record it once, keeping the fullest wording. Beyond that,
every goal, figure, stakeholder, date, decision, action item, open question, and risk in the
notes must appear here. Executive Summary is the only place to be brief. Omit sections 6
through 9 only when the notes truly have nothing for them; never write a filler line such as
"None recorded".

Stop after the closing assessment. Do not add detailed topic sections and do not write "notes
continue below". Do not emit a speaker-attribution disclaimer unless attribution is actually
impossible, and never claim the source ended mid-sentence.
