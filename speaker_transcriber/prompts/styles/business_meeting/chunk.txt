Write detailed notes for this transcript segment of a business meeting. These notes are kept as
the detailed record, so completeness matters more than brevity.

Cover only what is new in this segment. Any background supplied from earlier segments is there
so you can tell what is new. Never restate it, summarize it, or recap it.

## Structure

- Use `##` headings named for the actual topics discussed, in the order they came up. Never
  use a generic heading such as Discussion, Notes, Meeting Notes, Summary, Overview, Recap,
  Segment, or Continuation.
- Under each topic write compact Markdown bullets, one point per bullet, attributed inline to
  the speaker by the name or label the transcript uses. Do not write a participants list.
  The shape:

## Q3 Pricing Change

- Brian: the publisher wants a build by the end of the quarter; the contract depends on it.

- After the topics, add these headings only when this segment has material for them:
  `## Goals and targets` (objectives and the measures attached to them), `## Numbers`
  (every figure with its unit, period, and whether it is actual, forecast, or estimate),
  `## Budget and cost`, `## Stakeholders` (people, teams, partners, customers, and what each
  one wants), `## Timeline` (dates, milestones, and what depends on them), `## Decisions`,
  `## Commitments` (who committed to what, by when), `## Action items` (owner, action, and
  priority when stated), `## Questions`, and `## Risks, dependencies, and blockers`.
- Omit any heading you have nothing to put under. Never write a filler line such as "None".

## Content

Preserve every substantive point, target, figure, decision, commitment, request, concern,
question, answer, deadline, name, role, contract term, constraint, dependency, and blocker from
this segment. Keep the reasoning people gave and the disagreements between them. Mark a number
as an estimate when the speaker did. Drop only filler and verbatim repetition.

## Output

Start directly with the first `##` topic heading. Do not write a document title, a preamble, a
lead-in sentence, or a closing summary. Do not echo the `[MORE SEGMENTS FOLLOW]` or
`[END OF TRANSCRIPT]` marker. If more segments follow, do not claim the meeting ended.
