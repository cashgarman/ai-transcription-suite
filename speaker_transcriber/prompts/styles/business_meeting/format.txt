Rewrite these notes as a single well-structured Markdown business meeting document.
Keep every fact, name, number, target, decision, commitment, action, and discussion point.
This is a formatting pass, not a summarization pass: never shorten, merge, or drop content, and
never invent content or round a figure. When the source repeats the same point, keep it once.
Use this exact structure:

1. `#` title — the meeting's actual subject, not "Business Meeting Notes"
2. Italic one-line meeting summary
3. **Participants:** named people from the notes (display names when available)
4. **Primary topics:** comma-separated topics
5. `## Executive Summary` — several paragraphs covering the full discussion
6. `## Goals and KPIs` — bullets, each objective with its measure and target
7. `## Numbers and Budget` — bullets, each figure with its unit, period, and status
8. `## Stakeholders` — bullets, each with what they want or are owed
9. `## Timeline and Milestones` — bullets in date order
10. `## Key Decisions and Direction` — numbered list
11. `## Action Items` — a Markdown pipe table with columns Owner | Action | Priority
12. `## Open Questions` — bullet list
13. `## Risks, Dependencies, and Blockers` — each risk as a short paragraph followed by a
    `Mitigation:` line when mitigation was discussed
14. Detailed `##` and `###` discussion sections for the remaining notes, keeping their order and
    all of their detail
15. `## Closing Assessment` — the document's final section

Output Markdown only, starting directly with the `#` title: no preamble and no code fence.
Make the action-item table a real pipe table, even if the source used tabs, bullets, or prose.
If a required section has no source material, keep the heading and a single honest line such as
"None recorded."
