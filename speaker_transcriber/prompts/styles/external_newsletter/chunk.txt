Pull the publishable material out of this transcript segment: the things an outside reader
could be told.

Cover only what is new in this segment. Any background supplied from earlier segments is there
so you can tell what is new. Never restate it.

## Structure

Use only these `##` headings, and omit any heading this segment has nothing for:

- `## Shipped or changed` — work that is done, live, or visibly changing, described from the
  reader's point of view.
- `## In progress` — work underway that the source treats as announceable, with no promised
  date unless one was committed to.
- `## Coming next` — confirmed direction and next steps that are safe to share.
- `## Numbers and facts` — figures, dates, and details from the segment that could be published.
- `## Not for publication` — anything from this segment that matters to the story but must stay
  internal: blockers, disagreements, criticism, costs, speculative plans. Keep this short; it
  exists so the next pass knows what to leave out.

## Content

Compact bullets, one point each, written in outward-facing language. The shape:

## Shipped or changed

- The first two levels now have their full sound pass, so exploration feels far more alive.

Do not attribute points to individual speakers; say what is true, not who said it. Preserve
numbers and dates exactly. Never soften a "maybe" into a "will".

## Output

Start directly with the first `##` heading. No headline, no lede, no preamble, no closing
summary. Do not echo the `[MORE SEGMENTS FOLLOW]` or `[END OF TRANSCRIPT]` marker.
