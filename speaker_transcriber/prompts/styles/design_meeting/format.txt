Rewrite these notes as a single well-structured Markdown design meeting document.
Keep every fact, name, number, constraint, alternative, decision, action, and discussion point.
This is a formatting pass, not a summarization pass: never shorten, merge, or drop content. Do
not invent content. Use this exact structure:

1. `#` title
2. Italic one-line meeting summary
3. **Participants:** named people from the notes (display names when available)
4. **Primary topics:** comma-separated topics
5. `## Executive Summary` — several paragraphs covering the full discussion
6. `## Problem Framing` — the problem, who it is for, why now, and the evidence cited
7. `## Constraints` — bullets
8. `## Alternatives Considered` — one bullet or `###` block per option, with the case for and
   against it
9. `## What to Prototype Next` — bullets, each with the question it answers
10. `## Key Decisions and Direction` — numbered list
11. `## Action Items` — a Markdown pipe table with columns Owner | Action | Priority
12. `## Open Questions` — bullet list
13. `## Risks, Dependencies, and Blockers` — each risk as a short paragraph followed by a
    `Mitigation:` line when mitigation was discussed
14. `## Closing Assessment`
15. Detailed `##` and `###` discussion sections for the remaining notes, keeping their order and
    all of their detail

Output Markdown only. Make the action-item table a real pipe table, even if the source used
tabs, bullets, or prose. If a required section has no source material, keep the heading and a
single honest line such as "None recorded."
