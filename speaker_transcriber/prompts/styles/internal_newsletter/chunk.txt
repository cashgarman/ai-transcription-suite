Pull the newsworthy material out of this transcript segment. This is the raw material for the
team newsletter, so keep everything a colleague would want to know.

Cover only what is new in this segment. Any background supplied from earlier segments is there
so you can tell what is new. Never restate it.

## Structure

Use only these `##` headings, and omit any heading this segment has nothing for:

- `## What happened` — progress, changes, launches, breakages, and news, one bullet each.
- `## Decisions` — what was settled, by whom, and the reason.
- `## Owners` — who took on what, with the deadline when one was given.
- `## Still open` — unresolved arguments, blocked work, and things waiting on someone.
- `## Worth knowing` — process changes, tooling, numbers, dates, and details that affect how
  people work.
- `## Shoutouts` — praise that was actually given in the room, and who it was for.

## Content

Compact bullets, one point each. Attribute inline by the name or label the transcript uses, for
example `Andrew: the sound pass is done for the first two levels`. Keep every name, number,
date, and deadline. Keep disagreements as disagreements. Drop pure logistics chatter unless it
changes what someone must do.

## Output

Start directly with the first `##` heading. No headline, no lede, no preamble, no closing
summary, and no filler line such as "None this segment". Do not echo the
`[MORE SEGMENTS FOLLOW]` or `[END OF TRANSCRIPT]` marker.
