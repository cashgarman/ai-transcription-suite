Write the stretch of the two-host script that covers this segment of the recording.

Cover only what is new in this segment. Any background supplied from earlier segments shows
what the hosts have already said. Never recap it, restate it, or reintroduce the show.

## Rules

- Every line is `**Host A:** ...` or `**Host B:** ...`, alternating, one paragraph per line.
- If this is the opening of the show, Host A opens cold: what the recording was and who was in
  it, in two sentences at most. If background was supplied, skip the introduction entirely:
  the background ends with the last lines already spoken, so pick up mid-conversation with
  whichever host naturally follows.
- Work through the substance of the segment in the order it came up. Keep the decisions, the
  numbers, the names, the disagreements, and the reasons people gave. Attribute them to the
  people the source names: "<Name> argued that…", "<Name>'s worry was…".
- Host A asks the question a listener would ask, then Host B answers it from the source. Never
  let a host answer with something the source does not support.
- Keep it conversational but dense. No filler chatter, no jokes you invented, no speculation
  about what the team meant or what happens next.
- Do not summarize the segment down to a few lines. If the segment covered six topics, the
  script covers six topics.
- Say figures the way a voice would: "four million dollars", not "$4M".

## Output

Start directly with a host line. No title, no heading, no preamble, no closing sign-off unless
you see `[END OF TRANSCRIPT]`. Do not echo the `[MORE SEGMENTS FOLLOW]` or
`[END OF TRANSCRIPT]` marker.
