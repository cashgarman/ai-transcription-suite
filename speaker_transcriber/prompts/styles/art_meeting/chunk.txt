Write detailed notes for this transcript segment of an art meeting. These notes are kept as the
detailed record, so completeness matters more than brevity.

Cover only what is new in this segment. Any background supplied from earlier segments is there
so you can tell what is new. Never restate it, summarize it, or recap it.

## Structure

- Use `##` headings named for the actual topics discussed, in the order they came up. Never
  use a generic heading such as Discussion, Notes, Meeting Notes, Summary, Overview, Recap,
  Segment, or Continuation.
- Under each topic write compact Markdown bullets, one point per bullet, attributed inline to
  the speaker by the name or label the transcript uses. Do not write a participants list.
  The shape:

## Forest Biome Colour Keys

- Ronny: the silhouette reads too soft at distance; push the shoulder line harder.

- After the topics, add these headings only when this segment has material for them:
  `## Visual direction` (mood, palette, lighting, shape language, style targets, and the exact
  adjectives used), `## References` (artists, films, games, images, and links named),
  `## Assets, characters, and shots` (each piece discussed, with its state), `## Feedback`
  (what was asked for on a specific piece, and by whom), `## Approvals` (what was signed off,
  what needs another pass, what was rejected), `## Decisions`, `## Action items` (owner, action,
  and priority when stated), `## Questions`, `## Risks and blockers`, and
  `## Production details` (software, versions, file names, resolutions, formats, deadlines).
- Omit any heading you have nothing to put under. Never write a filler line such as "None".

## Content

Preserve every substantive point, critique, reference, adjective, decision, request, concern,
question, answer, number, deadline, name, asset name, tool, constraint, and blocker from this
segment. Keep the reasoning people gave and the disagreements between them. Drop only filler
and verbatim repetition. Do not compress a long critique into a single sentence.

## Output

Start directly with the first `##` topic heading. Do not write a document title, a preamble, a
lead-in sentence, or a closing summary. Do not echo the `[MORE SEGMENTS FOLLOW]` or
`[END OF TRANSCRIPT]` marker. If more segments follow, do not claim the meeting ended.
