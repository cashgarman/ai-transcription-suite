Write only the front matter (overview) for an art meeting document. Your source is the numbered
`### Section` blocks below: notes on consecutive segments of one meeting. A section may itself
be an earlier draft of this overview; either way, treat its contents as source material and
produce one single document. Never copy a `### Section N` heading into your output.

The detailed discussion is appended automatically after your output: the same section notes,
merged by topic. Do not reproduce it here and do not write detailed topic sections.

Produce exactly this structure and nothing else:

1. `#` title — the meeting's actual subject, not "Art Meeting Notes". Never invent a date.
2. Italic one-line meeting summary
3. **Participants** — the people named in the section notes, gathered from the attributions on
   the bullets. Prefer display names when the notes have them.
4. **Primary topics** — comma-separated
5. `## Executive Summary` — a few paragraphs covering the arc of the whole discussion
6. `## Visual Direction` — where the look landed: mood, palette, lighting, shape language, and
   the specific adjectives and comparisons people used. Bullets or short paragraphs.
7. `## References` — bullets for every artist, film, game, image, or link named, with what each
   one was cited for
8. `## Assets, Characters, and Shots` — bullets, one per piece discussed, each with its current
   state
9. `## Feedback and Approvals` — bullets: what was asked for on each specific piece and by whom,
   what was approved, what needs another pass, what was rejected
10. `## Key Decisions and Direction` — numbered list, every decision in the notes
11. `## Action Items` — Markdown pipe table with columns Owner | Action | Priority, every action
    in the notes
12. `## Open Questions` — bullet list
13. `## Risks, Dependencies, and Blockers` — include mitigation when it was discussed
14. `## Closing Assessment` — short

Consecutive sections overlap: when the same reference, critique, decision, action, question, or
risk appears in more than one section, record it once, keeping the fullest wording. Beyond
that, every reference, piece of feedback, decision, action item, open question, and risk in the
notes must appear here. Executive Summary is the only place to be brief. Omit sections 6
through 9 only when the notes truly have nothing for them; never write a filler line such as
"None recorded".

Stop after the closing assessment. Do not add detailed topic sections and do not write "notes
continue below". Do not emit a speaker-attribution disclaimer unless attribution is actually
impossible, and never claim the source ended mid-sentence.
