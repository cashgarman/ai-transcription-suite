Record the stand-up updates in this transcript segment.

Cover only what is new in this segment. Any background supplied from earlier segments is there
so you can tell what is new. Never restate it.

## Structure

- `## Updates` — one `###` per person who gave an update, named as the transcript names them.
  Under each person, use only these bullets, and only when they said something for them:

### Priya

- Done: merged the retry logic for the upload service (TICKET-482).
- Doing: migrating the settings screen to the new layout system.
- Blocked: waiting on Marco for staging database access.

- `## Announcements` — things said to the whole team: schedule changes, releases, process
  changes, reminders.
- `## Follow-ups` — work handed to someone, or a conversation explicitly taken offline, with
  the owner named.
- `## Shoutouts` — praise actually given, and who it was for.

Omit any heading with nothing under it.

## Content

One bullet per point. Keep ticket numbers, branch names, file names, dates, and numbers exactly
as spoken. If someone described several separate pieces of work, give each its own bullet under
the right label. Do not merge two people's updates, and do not invent a `Done`, `Doing`, or
`Blocked` line that was not given.

## Output

Start directly with `## Updates` or the first heading you have material for. No title, no
preamble, no summary, and no filler line such as "No blockers". Do not echo the
`[MORE SEGMENTS FOLLOW]` or `[END OF TRANSCRIPT]` marker.
