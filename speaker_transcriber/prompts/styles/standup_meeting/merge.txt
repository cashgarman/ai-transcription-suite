Write the stand-up notes. Your output is the whole document. Your source is the numbered
`### Section` blocks below: notes on consecutive segments of one stand-up. A section may itself
be an earlier draft of these notes; either way, treat its contents as source material and
produce one single document. Never copy a `### Section N` heading into your output.

Produce exactly this structure:

1. `#` title — `Stand-Up Notes`, or the team or project name plus `Stand-Up` when the notes
   make it clear. Never invent a date.
2. An italic one-line summary of where the team is.
3. `## Team Themes` — three to five bullets: what most of the team is pushing on, and the
   patterns across the updates. This is the only place you may generalize.
4. `## Updates` — one `###` per person, in the order they first spoke. Under each, only the
   bullets they earned: `- Done:`, `- Doing:`, `- Blocked:`. Merge everything that person said
   across all sections into their one block; a person must not appear twice, and a bullet
   repeated across sections appears once.
5. `## Blockers` — a Markdown pipe table with columns Owner | Action | Priority, one row per
   blocker or follow-up that needs someone to act. Priority is High only when the source
   treated it as urgent.
6. `## Announcements` — bullets. Omit if there were none.
7. `## Shoutouts` — bullets. Omit if there were none.

Every person who gave an update must appear exactly once, and every blocker in the notes must
appear in both that person's block and the Blockers table.

Keep it short and scannable. No paragraphs outside the one-line summary, no executive summary,
no closing assessment, no filler such as "None reported". Never invent an update or a blocker.

Output Markdown only, starting directly with the `#` title: no preamble and no code fence. Do
not append the raw discussion notes.
