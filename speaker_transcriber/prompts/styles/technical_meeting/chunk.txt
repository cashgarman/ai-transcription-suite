Record the technical substance of this transcript segment. These notes are the source material
for a decision record, so completeness matters more than brevity.

Cover only what is new in this segment. Any background supplied from earlier segments is there
so you can tell what is new. Never restate it.

## Structure

Use only these `##` headings, and omit any heading this segment has nothing for:

- `## Context` — the system, component, or situation under discussion, and its current state.
- `## Problem` — what is broken, blocked, or being chosen between, stated concretely.
- `## Options` — one `###` per option that was floated, however briefly. Under each, bullets for
  how it would work, its cost, its risk, who argued for or against it, and why. For example:

### Precompile shaders in CI

- Cash: moves the failure to build time, where it is visible; adds roughly four minutes per build.

- `## Decisions` — what was actually settled, and the reason given.
- `## Consequences` — what the choice commits the team to: migrations, rewrites, dependencies,
  performance implications, work that becomes unnecessary.
- `## Open Questions` — unresolved technical questions, with any partial answers given.
- `## Technical Details` — versions, file names, paths, APIs, error text, measurements,
  thresholds, hardware, deadlines, and constraints that do not belong to a single option.

## Content

Compact bullets, one point each. Attribute technical positions inline by the name or label the
transcript uses. Keep the numbers, the exact identifiers, and the reasoning people gave. Keep
disagreements as separate bullets rather than merging them into a consensus that did not
happen. Drop scheduling talk and banter.

## Output

Start directly with the first `##` heading. No title, no preamble, no closing summary, and no
filler line such as "None raised in this segment". Do not echo the `[MORE SEGMENTS FOLLOW]` or
`[END OF TRANSCRIPT]` marker.
