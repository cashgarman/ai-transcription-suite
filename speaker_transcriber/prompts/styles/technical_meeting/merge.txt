Write the decision record. Your output is the whole document. Your source is the numbered
`### Section` blocks below: notes on consecutive segments of one discussion. A section may
itself be an earlier draft of this record; either way, treat its contents as source material
and produce one single document. Never copy a `### Section N` heading into your output.

Produce exactly this structure:

1. `#` title — the technical subject, for example `Build Pipeline: Shader Compilation Strategy`.
2. An italic one-line statement of what was decided, or that no decision was reached.
3. `## Context` — the system and situation, in a short paragraph or two.
4. `## Problem` — what had to be decided, and the constraints on it.
5. `## Options Considered` — one `###` per option, each with a short description followed by
   bullets for its tradeoffs, with positions attributed to the people who argued them.
6. `## Decision` — what was chosen and the reasoning. Use a numbered list when the meeting
   settled several things. If nothing was decided, write that plainly and say what is blocking
   the decision.
7. `## Consequences` — what the decision commits the team to, including work created, work
   avoided, and new dependencies or risks.
8. `## Open Questions` — bullets, with any partial answers.
9. `## Technical Details` — versions, file names, paths, APIs, measurements, thresholds, and
   deadlines from the notes.
10. `## Follow-ups` — a Markdown pipe table with columns Owner | Action | Priority, only for
    work that was actually assigned or stated. Omit this section entirely when none was.

Consecutive sections overlap: when the same option, decision, question, or figure appears in
more than one section, record it once, keeping the fullest wording, and merge duplicate `###`
option blocks into one. Beyond that, every option, decision, consequence, open question, and
technical figure in the notes must appear. Context is the only place to be brief.

Omit any section with no material rather than writing "None". Never invent a benchmark, a
version, a constraint, or a decision that was not made. Do not append the raw discussion notes.

Output Markdown only, starting directly with the `#` title: no preamble and no code fence
around the document.
