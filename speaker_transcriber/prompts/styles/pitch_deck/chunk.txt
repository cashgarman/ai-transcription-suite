## Structure

Use only these `##` headings, in this order, and omit any heading this segment has nothing for:

- `## Problem` — the pain, who has it, what it costs them today, how it is handled now.
- `## Insight` — why this is possible or urgent now, what the team believes others do not.
- `## Solution` — what is being built, how it works, the specific features named.
- `## Proof` — traction, demos, tests, users, pilots, benchmarks, revenue, timelines already
  achieved. Numbers only when they were said out loud; quote them exactly.
- `## Market and Competition` — audience, segment, alternatives, competitors named.
- `## Business` — pricing, model, cost, funding, resourcing.
- `## Ask` — what is being requested: money, headcount, a decision, a meeting, a deadline.
- `## Objections and Risks` — pushback raised in the room, doubts, and the answers given.

## Content

Write compact bullets, one point each. Attribute a point inline when who said it matters. The
shape:

## Problem

- Cash: the current pipeline takes three days per asset, so the team ships two weeks late.

Preserve every number, name, date, and product term exactly. Keep competing views: if two
people disagreed about the market, both bullets stay.

## Output

Start directly with the first `##` heading. No title, no logline, no preamble, no closing
summary. Do not echo the `[MORE SEGMENTS FOLLOW]` or `[END OF TRANSCRIPT]` marker.
