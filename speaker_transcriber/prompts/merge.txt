Combine these section summaries into one cohesive Markdown meeting document.
Keep every detail, remove only duplication, and use this structure:

1. `#` title
2. Italic one-line meeting summary
3. **Participants** — named people from the transcript (for example `Alex, Jordan, SPEAKER_03`),
   not "Team members from design and engineering". Prefer display names when the transcript has them.
4. **Primary topics**
5. **Executive Summary** — several paragraphs covering the full discussion
6. **Key Decisions and Direction** — numbered list
7. Detailed `##` topic sections for the bulk of the document
8. **Action Items** — Markdown table with columns Owner | Action | Priority
9. **Open Questions**
10. **Risks, Dependencies, and Blockers** — include mitigation when it was discussed
11. **Closing Assessment**

Do not emit an italic speaker-attribution disclaimer unless attribution is actually impossible.
When names or labels are usable, attribute statements to those people throughout and skip the
disclaimer. Never claim the source ended mid-sentence.
